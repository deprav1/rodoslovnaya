# Genealogy Dead-Ends & Archival Strategy (2026-08-17)

## Problem
Hit a "brick wall" in online genealogical research for Pyotr Semyonov (Omsk region) and the Semyonov branch. Standard databases (Pamyat Naroda, Gwar, SudAct, nomer.org) were exhausted or protected (Cloudflare), and no digitized household registers (домовые книги) from the 1990s are publicly available. 

## Root Cause
Russian archival data for the 20th century (especially post-1920s) is strictly protected by privacy laws (100-year rule) and is not digitized or indexed on the open web. Attempting to force online searches leads to AI hallucinations or false positives (e.g., namesake in KAMAZ-master).

## Solution
Transition from OSINT/online searches to formal archival inquiries.
1. Created templates for ZAGS (Справка о смерти с иными сведениями) to retrieve the exact birthplace and parentage.
2. Created templates for GIAOO (State Archive of Omsk Oblast) to request extracts from house registers (ул. Бархатова).

## Prevention
When a genealogical search enters the "Soviet era blind spot" (1930s-1990s) without precise geographical coordinates (village/district), stop burning cycles on web searches. Immediately pivot to drafting ZAGS/Archive request templates for the user, as this is the only legally and historically sound method to proceed.
